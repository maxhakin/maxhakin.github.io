Legacy

A novel 4x strategy game in development for Windows and Mac. The game features a more tactical scope. This is it's development blog

Scope

Legacy is a turn based strategy game set on a procedurally generated hex planet. Its terrain shapes every decision: mountains create barriers and vantage points, rivers channel armies toward crossings, and cities grow across the land as players build quarters and improvements.

You begin with a people whose identity is still taking shape. The choices you make in settlement, research, trade and war move your civilisation along different paths, unlock its civics and influence how it is remembered. At each age transition, the game reflects that history through your civilisation’s standing, its heir or revolution, and the deeds that may become legendary.

Legacy brings together tactical, map-driven warfare and a history that grows from the player’s own choices. Its world is invented, but its technologies and civic development follow an earth-adjacent progression. The long-term contest is for legacy: when the world ends in a cataclysm, the civilisation with the greatest legacy wins—unless conquest decides the outcome first.

The game is being built in Godot with a separate C# rules library designed for repeatable simulation and future multiplayer. The first public release is planned for single-player on Steam.

Roadmap

Built: the tactical combat prototype

The prototype tested whether terrain could make battles rewarding. It introduced the spherical hex map, height and river systems, armies of up to three units, tactical combat, local hotseat play and a basic computer opponent. The prototype is complete and has been playtested.

In progress: the one-age vertical slice

The current phase is building a playable section of the full game, from starting a new planet through its first age transition. It already includes the main settlement and growth loop, contested territory, war and peace, research, civilisation identity, civics, heirs and revolutions, and legendary deeds. Barbarians, city-states and trade with them are also part of the slice.

The next work is to bring the remaining pieces together: complete the computer opponents and front end, refine presentation and audio, then tune and playtest the slice end to end. The slice is intended to prove that terrain, player choices and a civilisation’s emerging identity form one satisfying loop.

Planned: first release

The first release is planned as a single-player game on Steam. It will expand the slice across all ages, add the legacy victory and tracker, and build toward a world-ending cataclysm with survival routes. It will also include computer opponents designed to understand terrain, armies and territory, plus saving and loading, tutorials, and release-quality art and audio.

Later possibilities

Online multiplayer is planned after the single-player release. Other longer-term possibilities include the full royal dynasty system, curated maps, additional cataclysms, a space-age layer, and buildings that reshape the land. These are later ambitions, and their timing may change as the core game develops.
