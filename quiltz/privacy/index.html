Quiltz privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

This policy explains what information the Quiltz game for iPhone uses, why, and the choices you have. Quiltz is made by DAX Interactive ("we", "us"), based in the United Kingdom. Contact: dax.interactive.support@gmail.com.

In short:

  • We do not run any servers. We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and we never receive your personal information.
  • Your settings, saved game and purchase record stay on your iPhone.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some information from your device to show and measure those ads. You choose whether they can be personalised, and you can change your mind at any time.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Buying Remove Ads (or any theme pack) switches the ads off for good.

Information kept on your iPhone

The game stores a few small files in its own private storage on your device. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are deleted when you delete the game.

WhatWhy
Your settings (sound, music volume, motion, text size, theme, last game choices, whether you have seen the tutorial)So the game remembers them.
One unfinished gameSo you can carry on where you left off.
A list of what you have boughtSo Remove Ads and theme packs keep working when you are offline. Apple's records are always checked first.
How many games you have finished and when you last saw a full-screen adSo ads are shown no more than the game's limits allow (never in your first two games, then at most once every two games and three minutes).

Ads (Google AdMob)

If you have not bought Remove Ads, the game shows a banner on its menu screens and, now and then, a full-screen ad between games. There are no ads during a game. These ads are provided by Google's AdMob service.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game collects information from your device, which may include:

  • Device identifiers: Apple's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking, see below) and identifiers Google uses within this app.
  • Approximate location: worked out from your internet (IP) address. The game never asks for, or uses, your precise location.
  • Ad interactions: which ads were shown, and whether you tapped them.
  • Diagnostics: crash and performance information about the ad software.

Google uses this information as described in its own policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may process it outside the UK, including in the United States, with safeguards required by UK data protection law.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent (UK, European Economic Area and Switzerland): the first time you open the game, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised and how your information may be used. If you say no, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change your answer at any time in the game: Settings > Privacy choices.
  • Tracking (Apple): iPhone asks whether Quiltz may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google does not receive your advertising identifier. You can change this in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Remove Ads: buying Remove Ads (or any theme pack) in the game's Shop switches off all ads, and Google's ad software stops requesting them.

Ads are limited to those suitable for general audiences.

Purchases (Apple)

In-app purchases (Remove Ads, and any theme packs) are made through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment and your Apple Account; we never see your card details, name or email. The game only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics of our own.
  • We do not sell, share or receive your personal information.
  • The game does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location.

Children

Quiltz is a puzzle game for a general audience. It is not designed for children under 13 and we do not knowingly allow personal information about children to be collected. Ads are limited to general-audience content. If you believe a child's information has been collected through the game's ads, contact us and we will help you ask Google to remove it.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over your personal information, including the right to access, correct or delete it, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent at any time (see "Your choices about ads" above).

Because we hold no personal information about you, most requests are best made to Google (for ad data: Google's privacy help) or Apple (for purchases). You can delete everything the game keeps on your iPhone by deleting the game. If you need help, email us at dax.interactive.support@gmail.com.

If you are unhappy with how your information is handled, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes what it collects (for example, when online play is added), we will update this policy and the date at the top before the update reaches the App Store.
