Quiltz

A cosy patchwork puzzle game for iPhone. Pick fabric patches from the baskets, fill your rows and stitch them into your quilt.

In testing · coming to the App Store

How it plays

  1. Pick your fabric

    Take every patch of one fabric from a basket, or from the scrap table where the leftovers gather.

  2. Fill your rows

    Lay them along one of your five stitching rows. Patches that won't fit fall into your offcuts and cost points.

  3. Stitch your quilt

    Full rows are sewn into your quilt at the end of each round, scoring for every patch they join up with.

Made to feel like a sewing table

Cloth you can almost feel

Printed-cotton patches, linen boards, wicker baskets and a cutting mat, all with visible stitching.

Scoring, sewn in front of you

A needle stitches each new patch in, and a thread runs along every line it scores for.

Up to four players

Play one to three computer opponents at three difficulty levels, with a tutorial to learn the ropes.

Comfortable for everyone

Reduced-motion and text-size settings, and fabrics that differ in pattern as well as colour.

Coming next: play together
