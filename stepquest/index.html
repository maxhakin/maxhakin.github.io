StepQuest

A tower-defence game for iPhone. The steps you walk earn the coins that build your defences.

Each month is a new season: one battlefield and 20 levels, where your defence carries over from level to level, so walking builds one fortress over the month.

Your steps are read from Apple Health and turned into coins on your phone. They never leave it.

StepQuest is in testing and isn't on the App Store yet.
