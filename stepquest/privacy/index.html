StepQuest privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026. This policy covers the test version of StepQuest, shared through Apple's TestFlight. It will be reviewed before StepQuest is released to the public.

In short: your steps never leave your phone. The whole game works without an account. If you choose to play online, we keep only a made-up username, an age band and your battle points, so you can appear on the season leaderboard. You can delete that account in the game at any time.

Who we are

StepQuest is made by DAX Interactive. If you have a question about this policy or your information, email us at dax.interactive.support@gmail.com.

Your steps stay on your phone

With your permission, StepQuest reads your daily step counts from Apple Health and turns them into coins on your phone. Steps you typed into the Health app by hand are left out.

  • Step counts are never sent to us or anyone else, and are never used for advertising.
  • The game's record of steps already turned into coins is kept out of iCloud and device backups.
  • You can turn off StepQuest's access to Apple Health at any time in your iPhone's Settings. The game still works; you just won't earn coins from walking.

What's kept on your phone

Your game (levels, towers, coins, badges and settings) is saved on your phone. Like other app data it may be included in your phone's own backups. It isn't sent to us.

Playing online (optional)

To appear on the season leaderboard you can choose to play online. You sign in with Apple, and we don't ask Apple for your name or email address. Apple gives us a code that identifies you only to StepQuest.

When you play online, we keep:

Each level you win: the season, the level, the castle health left, the points and the timeTo work out your points for the leaderboard

Other signed-in players can see your username and your total points for the season. Nothing else about you is shown.

We never collect your steps, coins, real name, email address, date of birth, school, location, contacts or advertising identifier.

Children

StepQuest is made for children and young teenagers. Before a player under 13 can play online, a parent or carer must agree on the phone; until then, nothing about them is sent to us and they play offline as normal. Everyone taking part in this test does so with a parent's or guardian's agreement.

Where your information is kept

Online information is kept by our service provider, Supabase, on servers in Ireland (in the European Union). Supabase stores it for us and doesn't use it for anything else. Its servers also keep short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service.

TestFlight is run by Apple. Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us, under Apple's own privacy policy.

No ads, no tracking

StepQuest has no adverts, no in-app purchases, and no analytics or tracking tools, and it never sends you reminders to play.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your online account is kept until you delete it. To delete it, open the game, go to Profile and tap Delete account. This immediately removes your account, username and points from our servers, and starts the game on that phone again from the beginning.

When testing ends, we'll delete all test accounts.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing dax.interactive.support@gmail.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
