How we work

03

Decisions written down

Every significant choice goes in a decision log with the reason for it, so you always know why the product is the way it is.

Mockups before code

Two or three options for each screen, compared on a real device. A change of mind costs minutes, not days.

Tests that grow with the product

Each milestone adds tests, and the whole suite passes before moving on. Working fast never means cutting corners.

Honest progress

Each phase ends with real-device testing, screenshots and a clear list of what has been checked and what has not.
